From the scanner source

What a scan actually enumerates

  • Derived Data ~/Library/Developer/Xcode/DerivedData — marked safe for quick clean
  • Diagnostic reports ~/Library/Logs/DiagnosticReports — marked safe for quick clean
  • User caches ~/Library/Caches — marked safe for quick clean
  • Old large downloads Files in Downloads older than 21 days and at least 250 MB
  • Stale project weight Old large folders the scanner flags for review, not auto-trash

What the dashboard can do

  1. Scan now and show candidate count plus recoverable bytes
  2. Quick-clean only the safe-cache set (moves items to Trash)
  3. Trash selected rows after you choose them
  4. Optional auto-scan interval from 15 minutes to 24 hours

The app runs as a menu-bar extra. Nothing here claims how many gigabytes your machine will recover.
